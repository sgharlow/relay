## What counts as a "meeting"

Written down before the first one, so the number cannot be argued into existence later:

- **Counts:** a scheduled call or video meeting with someone at the partner who could sponsor or
  block a pilot, held.
- **Does not count:** a reply, a referral to a form, an intro to somebody who then does not reply,
  a conference conversation, or a meeting that was booked and did not happen.

That bar is deliberately higher than "a response". G1's own history is the reason: an instrument
that counts the easy event produces a number that looks like progress and decides nothing.
